## Visual language

- No frame, corner labels or scene numbers: the picture is only the scene.
- Type: Space Grotesk for kinetic type, JetBrains Mono for labels (or the product's, set in `theme.css`).
- The product's ground (`--pv-ground`) with brand-color washes that change per scene; a technical grid.
- Uppercase kinetic type; no plates: text lives over the scene.
- Pills that swap their word on every hit.
- Real screens of the product as 3D planes and cropped fragments toward the camera (fictional demo data).
- Velocity-matched cuts: zoom-through, whip with a streak, a flash on the strong cuts.
- Accents: action = CTA/success/new, data = numbers, brand = the product name, special = one reserved concept.

## Scenes

1. **Hook.** What we see, what the pill says, which fragments orbit, how it ends.
2. **Section.** Real sidebar + click; title; pill with 3 ideas; real plane; fragment toward the camera; the action moment.
3. **Finale.** One cut per beat under the closing line; logo + name + URL; fade to black.
