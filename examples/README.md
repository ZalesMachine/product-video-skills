# Examples

## `acme-desk/` · launch video of a fictional helpdesk

A small demo app (Acme Desk) and a 36-second launch video of it in English and Spanish: hook with orbiting fragments, a Dashboard section with numbers that count and hit, a Tickets section with a list in cascade and a click that solves a ticket, and a closing montage with the lockup. It runs on any machine (the music is a generated placeholder beat) and **is the visual bar of this repo**: before starting a new video, look at its frames and read its storyboard.
