This video tells support teams that **Acme Desk puts every ticket, number and customer in one desk**.

## Visual language

- No frame, corner labels or scene numbers: the picture is only the scene.
- Space Grotesk 700 uppercase for kinetic type; JetBrains Mono for labels and the URL.
- The app's ground (`--bg`, mapped to `--pv-ground`) with washes that change per scene; a technical grid.
- Pills that swap their word on a beat; real screens of Acme Desk as 3D planes and fragments toward the camera.
- Accents mapped from the app: action = `--accent` (yellow: CTA, the solved ticket, the CSAT number), data = `--info` (cyan: numbers and the section pills), brand = `--brand` (iris: the product name), special = `--chat` (pink: the chat channel only, never as a generic accent).
- Cuts: zoom-through as the main cut, one whip to change topic into the closing montage.
