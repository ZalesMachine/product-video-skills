# Reference · code-generated motion design posts

A set of short social clips (720×900, 14 to 32 s) showing motion design **generated by code from a single prompt** by AI models, with the prompt visible above each clip. They are a third party's posts and are not included here; this is what they teach.

## What they teach (and what was taken)

- **Cuts to the rhythm of imaginary music.** They all change idea every 1 to 2 seconds and never stop. Here that became literal: the grid of a real track and scenes in beats.
- **One idea per frame, full screen.** One huge word, one object, one background color. Hence kinetic type at 196 px and washes that change per scene.
- **Type as the lead**: words that enter letter by letter, repeat in strips, get underlined, with heavy weights and negative tracking. `PV.chars`, `PV.streakIn`, `PV.slam`, `.kt`.
- **Motion explains motion**: drawn ease curves, "stretch" vs "snap", follow-through. That is the reason for the "long-tail curves, no bounce" rule: the long tail is the signature here, and bounce never appears.
- **Real UI components, animated** (buttons that transform, toggles, sliders, stats that count, command palettes being typed, with a cursor). Here the UI is the product's own, captured, and the animation goes on top: cursor with click and ripple, a selection that grows, a highlighted button, counters.
- **Full-color background changes** as a cut. In a dark language that resource becomes a single **light contrast scene** in the hook and the washes that change per scene.
- **A clean final lockup**: name + role + thin line, still. Here: logo + name with glow + hairline frame + URL, fading out with the music.

## What wasn't taken

- Abstract shapes without meaning (particle spirals, cube fields, 3D tubes): in a product video they read as filler. The only "shapes" here are real fragments of the app.
- Glitch and RGB split.
- A light ground as the default.
- 15 seconds as a rule: a launch that shows every section of a product needs longer; social cuts come out of the master.

## Studying a new reference

```bash
ffmpeg -v error -y -i ref.mp4 -vf "fps=1,scale=240:-1,tile=8x2" -frames:v 1 ref-sheet.jpg
```

A contact sheet at one frame per second is enough to read the structure. Note: rhythm (changes per second), how many ideas, which techniques (type, shape, camera, color), how it closes, and what you would do differently. Never commit someone else's video.
